Meet Sara

An artist's eye, a professional's hands.

Sara Hakobyan is a Los Angeles based makeup artist and certified K-tip hair extension specialist working out of Beauty Fabric USA. Her work spans soft bridal glam, bold editorial looks, backstage fashion, music videos, feature films and full special-effects character transformations.

Every face is different. Sara starts with your skin, your features and the light you will be in, then builds a look that feels like you on your best day.

  • Bridal & event makeup
  • Film, TV & music video sets
  • Special effects & character work
  • Certified K-tip extensions
  • On-location & in-studio
  • English, Armenian, Russian spoken
